\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Between a prediction and an action lie three steps, and Know2Act measured each. Every target we supervised, up to the shape of the correct answer, is acquired and transfers to unseen databases; it enters the action policy only when written into the action; and it is usable at once from outside, as a selector. The agent's own decision is the step that fails: with model and context held fixed, it ignores both the contract and its own check, and outcome-only RL does not close the gap; that probably requires rewarding the use of knowledge.
