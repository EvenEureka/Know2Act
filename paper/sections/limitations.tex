\section*{Limitations}

The study uses one model family and size (Qwen3-4B), supervised fine-tuning only, and a single counterfactual environment derived from ALFWorld. Reinforcement learning after fine-tuning could change how much the prediction is used, as \citet{liu2026cotgains} report for reasoning traces.

The predictions are one-line templates that name a receptacle type, and the action names a receptacle of that type. Following such a prediction can be implemented as copying, and the high flip effect for randomly ordered predictions is consistent with that. Free-form or paraphrased predictions may behave differently.

The conventions are arbitrary by construction, and the teachers are scripted searchers. Both choices make the knowledge identifiable but less natural than the knowledge of a deployed agent.

The secondary arms have three seeds each, so the contrasts H3 and H4 have little power. The imitator reference is only bounded for \arm{N100}, \arm{N100M} and \arm{SA50v2}, so their copy biases are intervals.

The first decision point was chosen as the primary estimand after a validation pilot and was then confirmed on the held-out test split.
